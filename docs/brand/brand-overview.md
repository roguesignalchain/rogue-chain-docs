# Rogue Signal Brand Identity

Rogue Signal represents a sovereign, high-performance decentralized protocol governed by the **RandomX (`rx/0`)** CPU-first Proof-of-Work consensus. The visual language embodies **High-Contrast Tactical Monochrome Brutalism**—a philosophy rooted in cypherpunk principles, engineering rigor, and stark photographic telemetry.

---

## The Monochromatic Paradigm

Unlike generic Web3 design systems laden with pastel gradients and neon noise, Rogue Signal strictly adheres to an uncompromising black-and-white visual identity:

* **The Void (`#000000` / `#050505`):** The primary universe. Interfaces are built upon deep obsidian surfaces that maximize contrast, conserve energy on OLED hardware, and focus cognitive attention solely on functional telemetry.
* **Piercing Luminance (`#FFFFFF`):** High-impact white is reserved for primary typography, vital status indicators, cryptographic hashes, and primary action buttons.
* **Tactical Grayscale (`zinc-400` through `zinc-800`):** Secondary metadata, 1px hairline dividers, reticle crosshairs, and structural wireframes occupy a disciplined spectrum of neutral grays.

---

## Core Aesthetic Influences

The Rogue Signal visual system is directly derived from the official promo series (`promo_*.png`):

1. **Brutalist Technical Grid:** Clean 1px orthogonal borders, strict geometric alignment, and tactical bracket notations (`[RX-0]`, `[ID: 800055]`, `[MTP-11]`).
2. **Hardware Telemetry HUD:** Telemetry-style metrics and status pills inspired by mission-critical avionics and sovereign UNIX terminal dashboards.
3. **Pure Typography:** Unyielding geometric clarity with **Space Grotesk** for commanding headlines and **JetBrains Mono** for cryptographically verifiable data.
