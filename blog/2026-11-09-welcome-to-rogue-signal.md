We are excited to launch the official documentation hub for **Rogue Signal (ROGUE)**.

<!-- truncate -->

Rogue Signal represents a decisive step toward truly decentralized, ASIC-resistant proof-of-work blockchain computing. Powered by **RandomX (rx/0)**, Rogue Signal brings consensus back to everyday CPU miners while providing a high-throughput, EVM-compatible smart contract execution layer.

### What is covered in our initial documentation?

* **Network Architecture:** Full parameters including the 8 ROGUE block reward, 95%/5% miner/treasury split, and 100M hard cap.
* **Core Node Setup:** Building and deploying `rogue-core-chain` natively across Linux, macOS, and Windows.
* **RandomX Mining:** Maximizing your processor efficiency with Huge Pages and CPU thread allocation.
* **Desktop Client:** Self-custodial security with AES-256-GCM vault architecture.

Stay tuned for upcoming network milestones, mining pool launches, and developer guides!
